A fictional technical note on parcel-locker door sensors follows.

Field review of the Harbor Point parcel-locker bank, unit cluster B, found that the door sensor failed to register closure in 17 of 240 compartment cycles sampled over six weeks, a fault rate of about 7.1 percent. Most failures clustered in the mornings, when humidity inside the enclosure was highest and the magnetic reed contacts showed visible condensation residue. Technicians at the Ferrow Lane depot, which maintains the bank under a contract with the fictional operator Lindqvist Terminals, attributed the pattern to seal degradation rather than wiring faults, though no teardown was performed. The sample covered only one bank in one climate zone, so the rate may not hold for lockers installed in drier or sheltered sites. The review also did not test the newer capacitive sensor model, which the operator is evaluating for the next procurement cycle. Before any sensor replacement is ordered, the operator should confirm that the seal failure reproduces under controlled humidity testing, since a repeat of the morning pattern would support the seal explanation and a different pattern would not.
