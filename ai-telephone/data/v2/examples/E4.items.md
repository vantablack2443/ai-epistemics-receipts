# E4 items (bakery oven temperature controller)

Finding: The principal finding is that replacing the stock controller's fixed integral gain with a gain scheduled by door-open events reduced peak temperature overshoot from 14 degrees Celsius to 6 degrees across 212 recorded loading cycles.

Evidence limit: Thermocouple readings were logged at one-second intervals, but the trial ran on a single oven, so the observed effect may reflect that unit's insulation and burner age rather than the design itself.

Scope limit: The results apply only to deck ovens running between 200 and 260 degrees Celsius, and they say nothing about convection or rotary models, which heat by different mechanisms.

Condition before acting: Before any bakery adopts the scheduled-gain firmware, its technician must confirm that the door sensor is wired to the controller's interrupt input, because a floating input produces spurious gain changes.
