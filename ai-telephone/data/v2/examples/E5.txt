Technical Note TN-2026-07: Relative Humidity Drift in the Mereton Gallery Storage Annex

Dr. Lorna Vashti and colleagues recorded relative humidity at eleven wall-mounted sensors in the Mereton Gallery storage annex across a nine-week window last autumn. The principal finding is that readings at the northeast corner rose by 14 percent relative humidity during the four nights following each weekly deep-clean, when the building's dehumidifier was switched off for maintenance. The sensors were calibrated against a reference hygrometer before deployment, but the reference unit itself was only checked once, which means absolute values may sit a point or two off even though the relative pattern holds. The study covered a single annex with one ventilation layout, so the same cleaning-related swing may not appear in galleries with different construction, occupancy, or climate control. Before anyone adjusts the cleaning schedule or the dehumidifier timing, the team should confirm that the reference hygrometer has been recalibrated against a traceable standard, since any shift in that instrument would change the size of the effect the note reports.
