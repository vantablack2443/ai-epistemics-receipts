## Introduction and related work

Engineering handoffs compress findings, evidence limits, and action conditions. We propose a controlled comparison of ordinary, preservation-instructed, and structured preservation summaries across three rounds with a direct-summary control. Kolagar and Zarcone study uncertainty transfer in summarization; Nnamdi and colleagues study epistemic erosion across professional text. This proposed study differs in its controlled engineering handoffs, explicit action conditions, sequential compression, and separation of preservation instructions from structure. It does not claim uncertainty loss is new.
